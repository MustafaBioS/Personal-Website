After a very long time, I finally decided to claim my own little corner of the giant place we call the internet. So I thought maybe it's finally time i make my first personal website.

As I'm writing this, I've had zero hours of sleep, two Monsters, and a coffee. I might be losing sentience...

I don't really have ANYTHING on my mind to write about right now. I'm mostly typing whatever comes to mind so I can make sure this whole blog thing actually works. I'm pretty sure i had some stuff i wanted to write about but i think i'm too sleep deprived to remember.

As ironic as it is coming from someone who's still awake at 10:30 in the morning, sleep really is important. I've lost count of how many times I've spent hours stuck on something, convinced it was some impossible problem, only to wake up later, look at it with fresh eyes, and think:

> I seriously spent all this time trying to figure out this simple problem...?

90% of the time it ends up being something stupidly simple.

Anyway i should probably stop larping being a writer and go to sleep.
